- 2026-10-15: The accepted candidates all came from repos with 0 to 16 stars, although the fit profile asks for 5,000+ stars first. Cause: ranking by "fewest comments first" favors tiny repos. I measured it on 973 open, unassigned "good first issue" issues (722 repos, star counts from `gh api`): median comments per issue is 5 for repos with 5,000+ stars, 3 for 500 to 4,999, and 0 for under 500. The cap of 3 dropped 68% of recent large-repo issues (8 of 25 kept), 31% of mid-size (18 of 26) and 4% of small (353 of 368). In a sample of 45 issues with 1 to 8 comments, about two thirds (28) had a claim-like comment (10 of 15 large, 11 of 17 mid, 7 of 13 small), so I kept the cap of 3 for the computer vision and local models fields. The sample is small and the claim match was a crude phrase search, so treat the numbers as rough.
